VR Video Streamer includes these free programs, unchanged. Each belongs to its own authors and keeps its own licence.
The licence texts are in app\tools\licenses.

Included in this folder
- MediaMTX (app\tools\mediamtx.exe)         MIT licence        https://github.com/bluenviron/mediamtx
- cloudflared (app\tools\cloudflared.exe)   Apache 2.0 licence https://github.com/cloudflare/cloudflared
- yt-dlp (app\tools\youtube\yt-dlp.exe)     Unlicense, with    https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp
                                            third-party parts listed in yt-dlp-THIRD-PARTY.txt
- Deno (app\tools\youtube\deno.exe)         MIT licence        https://github.com/denoland/deno

Downloaded later by this program, only after asking
- FFmpeg, builds from gyan.dev, GPL licence              https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/
- Whisper speech models from whisper.cpp, MIT licence    https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp
- 7-Zip's 7zr.exe, on older Windows only, then removed   https://github.com/ip7z/7zip

Services the program can connect to when you use the matching feature: Cloudflare, YouTube and
other video sites, OpenSubtitles, and Cloudflare's speed test. This program isn't made by or connected with any of
them.
